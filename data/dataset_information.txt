Dataset Name: APTOS 2019 Blindness Detection
Source: Kaggle (Aravind Eye Hospital, India)
Modality: Digital Retinal Fundus Photography (Topcon, Zeiss, Canon)
Total Samples: 3,662 retinal images (PNG format)
Classes: 5 Ordinal Stages
  - 0: No DR (1,805 samples, 49.29%)
  - 1: Mild (370 samples, 10.10%)
  - 2: Moderate (999 samples, 27.28%)
  - 3: Severe (193 samples, 5.27%)
  - 4: Proliferative DR (295 samples, 8.06%)
Partition Strategy: Stratified 70/15/15 Split
  - Train: 2,563 images (69.99%)
  - Validation: 549 images (14.99%)
  - Test: 550 images (15.02%)
Preprocessing: Ben Graham's method (border removal, local Gaussian contrast subtraction, 224x224 resize)
